## Users

Adults in Salvador, especially in and around Pituba, who are looking for advanced aesthetic care and want a clear, low-pressure way to understand the clinic and request an evaluation.

This is a premium demonstration landing page and link page for Clínica Vida e Beleza — Estética Avançada em Salvador. Its job is to make the clinic easier to understand online and turn interest into a WhatsApp conversation or evaluation request.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site must also provide a link page at `/links`.
- The project includes a commercial proposal prototype at `/proposta`, with PDF and editable DOCX downloads following the same visual direction.
- The page is a local commercial demonstration and must not be published without authorization.
- LGPD, privacy and cookie transparency are part of the minimum scope before publication; the final text, controller and channels must be approved by the clinic and reviewed legally when applicable.
- Do not invent procedures, testimonials, metrics, certifications, logos, clinical outcomes or team credentials.
- Use careful language about aesthetic care and never promise results.

## Product Principles

1. Make the first conversation feel easy and respectful.
2. Use specificity of place and next step as trust signals, not invented clinical proof.
3. Explain what happens next before asking for contact.
4. Keep the page elegant, legible and calm across desktop and mobile.
5. Treat every visual asset as a demonstration until the clinic approves it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, readable contrast, descriptive image alternatives, reduced-motion support and touch targets that remain comfortable on small screens.
